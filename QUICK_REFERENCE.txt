RISK SCORING (Lines 719-1004):
  RED flags (2-5 pts each):
    • Honeypot detected
    • Mint function exists
    • Blacklist function
    • Hidden owner
    • LP not locked & not burned (for non-official Clanker)
    • Buy/sell tax > 10%
    • Top 3 holders > 40% supply

  YELLOW flags (1 pt each):
    • isVerified = FALSE (3rd-party Clanker deploy)
    • Buy/sell tax 5-10%
    • LP partially locked
    • Deployer wallet < 20 transactions
    • Unverified source (non-Clanker only)
    • Top 3 holders 20-40% supply

  GREEN flags (0 pts, positive):
    • isVerified = TRUE (official Clanker)
    • LP locked or burned
    • Source code verified
    • No tax or low tax (0-5%)
    • Good holder distribution

  Verdict Calculation:
    Score 0-3   → LOW risk
    Score 4-7   → MEDIUM risk
    Score 8-14  → HIGH risk
    Score 15+   → EXTREME risk
